# Concept and how it works
Everything you set in Dash Pro (pages, layouts, the Lap Review and the alerts) is saved as one **Preset**. You can keep a single Preset for everything, or give a device, a sim, a car class or a car its own.

The row at the top of Dash Pro decides which Preset you're editing: **Device**, **Sim**, **Class** and **Vehicle**.

* **Ticked** means that level gets its own Preset. Choose which one to edit from its dropdown.
* **Unticked** means that level shares one Preset. For example, with Sim unticked every sim uses the same setup.

What you edit is what you see on the dash. Unticking a level only hides its Presets, so ticking it again brings them back.

## Managing your Device(s)
Open **Manage Devices & Controls** to add the SimHub devices you run Lovely Dash Pro on.

* **Add a device** from the list of your SimHub devices. Start it from a copy of **All Devices**, or from the pages Dash Pro comes with.
* **Set its paging controls**: each device has its own **Next Page** and **Previous Page** buttons.
* **Remove a device** to delete its Presets. This can't be undone.

With **Device** unticked, every device shows the same **All Devices** Preset.

## Selecting and targeting your sim, class and vehicle
Tick **Sim**, **Class** or **Vehicle** to target a setup more precisely, then pick the one to edit from its dropdown.

* **Sim** gives each sim its own Preset.
* **Class** gives each car class within a sim its own Preset.
* **Vehicle** gives a single car its own Preset. Cars appear in the list once you've driven them in the game.

On track, the dash uses the most specific Preset it finds: the car first, then its class, then the sim, then the one for all sims.

{: .highlight }
> Start simple. Build one Preset for everything, then tick a level only when a sim or a car needs something different.
