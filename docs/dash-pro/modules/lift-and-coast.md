# Lift & Coast
Le Mans Ultimate's lift and coast cue, as a bar.

<div class="dp-grid">

* The bar fills as the lift point gets closer.
* From the lift point it turns **purple** and blinks: lift now.
* Set where the lift point is in [Alerts & Notifications](../alerts.html), along with the LIFT NOW alert.
