# Pit Loss
Where you would rejoin if you pitted now, and what a stop costs at this track.

<div class="dp-grid">
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/pit-loss/module-290x134-pit-loss.png" alt="Main" width="290" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Main</figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/pit-loss/data-140x86-pit-loss.png" alt="Data" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Data</figcaption></figure>
</div>

* **In a race**: the position you'd rejoin in, with the places you'd lose.
* **What a stop costs** your class at this track: a pit stop, a drive-through, and each second standing still.
* **With a penalty to serve**: where you'd rejoin after serving it.
* **During your stop**: your live rejoin position, the time spent so far and the time still to come.
* The costs are learned from past sessions at the track. Until there's enough data, it shows N/A.

The [MFM](./mfm-pages.html) has a full Pit Loss page.
