# Fuel and Energy Bars
Your fuel, Virtual Energy and hybrid battery as bars along the Topbar or the Sidebar.

* **Fuel**: how full the tank is. It changes colour as the level drops and blinks when you're nearly out.
* **Virtual Energy** (Le Mans Ultimate): the Virtual Energy left.
* **Fuel / VE**: both bars together.
* **SoC**: the hybrid battery's charge. It turns red when nearly empty and green when nearly full.

In the Main grid and the Data column, Fuel and SoC are plain values.
