# Gaps
The time gaps to the cars around you: on track, or only the cars in your class.

* **Track Ahead / Track Behind**: the gap in seconds to the car directly ahead or behind you on track.
* **Class Ahead / Class Behind**: the same, for the nearest cars in your class.
* **Track Gaps / Class Gaps**: both gaps in one module.
* **On the Topbar**: the car ahead and the car behind with their position and name, each gap, and an arrow showing which way the gap is moving.
* On the wider Lovely Dash Pro formats, the Topbar also compares their best lap and 5 lap average with yours: red when theirs is faster, green when slower, purple for the session's best lap.
