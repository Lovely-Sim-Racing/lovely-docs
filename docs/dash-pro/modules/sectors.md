# Sectors
The sectors of the lap you're on, coloured as you complete them.

<div class="dp-grid">
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/sectors/data-140x86-current-lap-sectors.png" alt="Current Lap Sectors" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Current Lap Sectors</figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/sectors/topbar-sector-bar.png" alt="Sector Bar (Topbar)" width="758" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Sector Bar (Topbar)</figcaption></figure>
</div>

* One segment per sector of the track, in the colour of your time:
  * **Purple**: the fastest sector of the session, by anyone.
  * **Green**: your personal best.
  * **Yellow**: slower than your best.
  * **Dark**: not driven yet.
* **Sector Bar** spreads the segments across the Topbar, with each sector's time written in it and the sector you're in filling as you drive.

The sectors of the lap you just completed are in the [Lap Review](../lap-review.html).
