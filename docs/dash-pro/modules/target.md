# Target
How much fuel to use per lap to reach the finish, or to save a stop.

<div class="dp-grid">
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/target/module-140x134-target.png" alt="Main" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Main</figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/target/data-140x86-target.png" alt="Data" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Data</figcaption></figure>
</div>

* **No stops left**: the most fuel you can use per lap and still reach the flag.
* **Stops still to make**: what you'd need per lap to make one stop fewer. It stays grey while that's out of reach, and shows **-PIT** once it's within reach.
* The badge compares your last lap with the target.
* Its colour tells you whether the target is tight or comfortable.
* In Le Mans Ultimate it uses Virtual Energy when that runs out first. In F1, with no refuelling, it's always to the finish.
