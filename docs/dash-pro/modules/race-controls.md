# Race Controls
The race controls you use on track, lit while they're active.

<div class="dp-grid">
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/race-controls/data-140x86-race-controls.png" alt="Race Controls" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Race Controls</figcaption></figure>
</div>

* **DRS**: lit while DRS is available or open. Where activations are limited (RaceRoom), it shows how many are left.
* **OT**: F1's overtake mode, or push-to-pass in iRacing.
* **BOX** (F1): when worn or damaged tyres, brakes or wings call you in, or on your pit window lap.
