# Linear Map
A track map drawn as a straight line, centred on you: see who's around you on track at a glance.

<div class="dp-grid">
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/linear-map/topbar-linear-map.png" alt="Topbar" width="758" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Topbar</figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/linear-map/sidebar-linear-map.png" alt="Sidebar" width="28" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Sidebar</figcaption></figure>
</div>

* You're the diamond in the middle; each car nearby is a dot with its position, in its class colour.
* Cars sit at their time gap to you, so you can see a car closing in or dropping back.
* On the **Topbar** the cars ahead are to the right; on the **Sidebar** they're above you.
