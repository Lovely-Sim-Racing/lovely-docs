# Delta
Your live delta to your best lap of the session: how much you're gaining or losing on this lap, as you drive it.

* **Main and Data**: the delta in seconds, green while you're faster, red while you're slower.
* **Topbar and Sidebar**: a bar that grows from the middle towards the faster or the slower side, with the delta written on it.
