# Pit Window
The laps your next pit stop can be made on, and still reach the finish.

  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/pit-window/module-140x134-pit-window.png" alt="Main" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Main</figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/pit-window/data-140x86-pit-window.png" alt="Data" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Data</figcaption></figure>
</div>

* The window as a range of laps, for example **12–19**: pit at the end of any of them.
* **White** until it opens, **green** while it's open, **red** on its last lap.
* **NONE** when no stop is needed.
* In F1, the latest lap the game gives you for your stop.
* In Le Mans Ultimate, a **VE** tag shows when Virtual Energy sets the window.
