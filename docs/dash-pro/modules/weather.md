# Weather
The temperatures, the track's condition and what the weather is about to do.

* **Air** and **track** temperatures, with the track's condition as a tag: grip in ACC, wetness in iRacing and Le Mans Ultimate.
* When a temperature or the forecast changes, the new value shows big for a few seconds.
* The double size adds a row for your sim:
  * **ACC and F1**: the forecast now, in 10 minutes and in 30 minutes.
  * **Le Mans Ultimate**: how wet the track is.
  * **Automobilista 2**: the rain.
  * **iRacing**: the sky.
