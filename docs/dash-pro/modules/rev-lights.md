# Rev Lights
Shift lights along the Topbar or the Sidebar.

* **Virtual RPM**: LED shift lights that follow your car's real shift lights when the car is known, gear by gear. Other cars use the red line set in SimHub.
* **Virtual RPM - ATSR**: the same lights in the colours of ATSR Hub Evo. Only available when ATSR Hub Evo is installed.
* **Rev Bar** (Topbar): a bar that fills as the revs rise, from green to the red line.
