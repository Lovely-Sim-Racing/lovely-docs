# Refuel
The fuel to add at your next stop to reach the finish, or the fuel you'll have left at the flag.

<div class="dp-grid">
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/refuel/module-140x134-refuel.png" alt="Main" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Main</figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/refuel/data-140x86-refuel.png" alt="Data" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Data</figcaption></figure>
</div>

* **REFUEL** (yellow): while a stop is needed, the fuel to add to reach the finish, at most what fits in the tank.
* **FINISH**: when you can make it without stopping, the fuel left when you cross the line.
* When more than one stop is needed, it tells you how many.
* In Le Mans Ultimate, it works with Virtual Energy when that runs out before the fuel.
