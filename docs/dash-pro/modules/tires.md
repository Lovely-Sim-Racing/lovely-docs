Everything about one tire in a single module: pressure, temperatures and wear.

* The tire's **pressure**, **temperature** and **brake temperature**.
* A **wear** bar, with the wear left.
* The **compound**, where the sim reports it.
* Temperatures are coloured from cold to hot.
* Choose whether the pressure or the temperatures are the big number.
* The **AVG** modules show the same values averaged over your last laps, for setup work.

One module per tire: Front Left, Front Right, Rear Left and Rear Right.
