# Rotary Dials
The position of your wheel's rotary dials.

  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/rotary-dials/data-140x86-left-dial.png" alt="Left Dial (double)" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Left Dial (double)</figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/rotary-dials/data-140x38-left-dial.png" alt="Left Dial" width="140" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Left Dial</figcaption></figure>
</div>

* **Left Dial** and **Right Dial**: the label of the dial's current position, for example **BIAS**.
* The double size also shows the positions before and after it, and scrolls when you turn the dial.
* Works with Ascher Racing wheels and dashes, the Greywolf GWT-01, and Generic Control Mapper buttons.
* For an Ascher dial in encoder mode, it shows arrows instead of a label.

The Rotary Dials alert in [Alerts & Notifications](../alerts.html) pops up the dial's label when you turn it.
