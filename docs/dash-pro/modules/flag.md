# Flag
The flag you're under, as a bar along the Topbar or the Sidebar.

* Shows for as long as the sim shows the flag.
* **Topbar**: the bar fills with the flag's colour and its name.
* **Sidebar**: the bar fills with the flag's colour, and shows its pattern:
  * Sector yellows: only the flagged sectors light up (sector 1 at the top).
  * Meatball: three orange dots.
  * Black: a white outline.
  * Finish: the chequered pattern.
* Clear Alerts doesn't hide it.

The Flags alert in [Alerts & Notifications](../alerts.html) pops a new flag up over the gear.
