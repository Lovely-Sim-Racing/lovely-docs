# Utility Bar
Several modules in one Topbar.

<div class="dp-grid">
  <figure><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/dash-pro/modules/utility-bar/topbar-utility-bar.png" alt="Utility Bar" width="758" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Utility Bar</figcaption></figure>
</div>

* [Functions](./functions.html) and [Race Controls](./race-controls.html) tiles.
* **Lap**, **Position** (with places gained or lost), **Time Remaining**.
* **Track Time**: the time of day on track, when the sim reports it.
* [Weather](./weather.html).
* The wider Lovely Dash Pro formats add the session, your best and last lap. The widest also adds the class best lap, your incidents (iRacing) and your pit stops.
