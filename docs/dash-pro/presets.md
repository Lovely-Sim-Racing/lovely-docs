# Import, Export & Browse Presets
A Preset holds a level's pages, layouts, Lap Review and alert settings, and can be saved as a `.dashpro` file. The three buttons work on the level selected at the top of Dash Pro.

## Import
**Import** replaces the selected level's setup with a `.dashpro` file.

## Export
**Export** saves the selected level's setup as a `.dashpro` file, to back it up or share it.

## Browse Presets
**Browse Presets** opens the community presets. Search or filter by tag, open a preset to see its pages, and **Import** it into the selected level.

{: .highlight }
> Made a layout you're proud of? Use **Submit a preset** in Browse Presets to share it with everyone.
