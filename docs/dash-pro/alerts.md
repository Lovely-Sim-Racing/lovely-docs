# Alerts & Notifications
The **Alerts & Notifications** tab decides what pops up on the dash while you drive.

For each alert you can:

* **Turn it on or off.**
* **Choose its size**, such as **Strip** across the top, **Full Screen**, or **Small** over the gear.
* **Choose its colors**, **Normal** or **Inverted** (black background).
* **Set how long it stays**, where that applies.

Some alerts have their own settings, such as fuel warning levels or launch RPM.

## Alerts

* **Vehicle Settings**: when you change TC, ABS, brake bias, engine map and so on.
* **Fuel**: low fuel warnings and **Pit Now**.
* **Lap Review**: the [Lap Review](./lap-review.html) at the line.
* **Pit Limiter**: speed and gear while the limiter is on.
* **Engine & Ignition**: IGN OFF and ENG OFF.
* **Launch Mode**: launch RPM at a race start.
* **Lift & Coast**: LIFT NOW in Le Mans Ultimate.
* **Engine Warnings**: when the engine runs too hot or loses pressure.
* **Penalty**: until you serve your penalty.
* **Flags**: when a flag is out.
* **Damage**: when your damage gets worse.
* **Rotary Dials**: when you turn a supported rotary dial.

## Clear Alerts
Set a **Clear Alerts** button to hide the alerts on screen until they come back.
